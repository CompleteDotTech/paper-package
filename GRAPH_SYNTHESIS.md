# Graph-synthesis research and visual assessment

Start with the [illustrated research manuscript](graph_synthesis/paper.md), the [15-figure gallery](graph_synthesis/figures/README.md), and the [review of evidence and remaining limitations](graph_synthesis/VISUAL_REVIEW.md).

The assessment combines the frozen Jev observations, the executed durable-graph study, and the existing relationship-fusion controls. It distinguishes entity identity, document-to-claim evidence links, topology, confidence, and reversible graph updates. It does **not** claim an end-to-end graph-synthesis benchmark or a KARMA replication.

[Reproduction and PDF commands](graph_synthesis/README.md) regenerate the charts and manuscript without model calls. [Chart data](graph_synthesis/figures/data.json), [compact comparison CSV](graph_synthesis/figures/edge_summary.csv), and the [figure manifest](graph_synthesis/figures/MANIFEST.json) provide an audit trail. Original inventory files, observations and the historical manuscript are unchanged.
