The one-cluster control is in `tests/test_adaptive.py`. Failure-isolation mutations prohibit using adjudication after invalid prerequisites. The misleading-weight control in `H5.misleading_weight_control` intentionally selects a false assertion to expose the gap between constraint consistency and semantic truth.

`run.load()` validates the original multicall artifact manifest, reconstructs raw responses, and invokes its offline verifier before any new computation. H1-H3 intervals are paired source-group bootstrap percentiles with 4,000 draws; 95% and 99% intervals are descriptive, conditional on the fixed fit and selections, not simultaneous or confirmatory. Negative findings remain in the report. No default compiler policy is altered.

`artifact-manifest.json` binds this extension's source and results. `validation/` records execution and unit-test output; `environment.json` records the execution environment.
