# Five adaptive improvements: exploratory protocol

Baseline: a1555ade897a570be811039f4760ef17e55b793a (merged PR #14).

This protocol is committed before execution of the new suite. Earlier results, including the multicall test results, are already public and were inspected to form these hypotheses. This is an exploratory follow-on, not independent preregistration or a new held-out study. No new Jev service calls are planned. H1-H3 reconstruct policies from the authentic 3,024-call multicall panel; H4 simulates review on its observed edges; H5 tests a bounded graph optimizer against an independent finite oracle. Never pool these evidence types into a success rate.

## Common design

Preserve all original evidence. Verify the multicall plan, responses, predictions and source-group split before analysis. Fit H3 redundancy clusters and H4 error-risk tables using only the 73 development candidates; evaluate on all 263 test candidates in 149 source groups. Published test results are not fresh validation. Decision functions receive no gold labels. Count failures, abstentions, wrong polarity and missing edges explicitly. Rank accepted edges by declared score with candidate-ID tie-breaking for equal-volume comparisons; do not interpret scores as calibrated probabilities. Charge every required recorded call, including failed and preliminary calls, once per candidate and policy. Do not infer real routed latency, invoices or new inference from these counterfactual costs.

Use 4,000 paired source-group bootstrap draws, seed 20260919, for H1-H3 rate/precision/recall contrasts. Report descriptive 95% and wider 99% percentile intervals. These are not simultaneous intervals for all secondary endpoints, not equivalence tests, and do not cure prior test exposure. Keep fixed selections during resampling and disclose this conditioning. No test-based retuning or removal of unfavorable methods. A target pass below is only a point-estimate engineering criterion.

## H1: Compact disagreement-triggered escalation

Motivation: contrastive prompts saved tokens, while saturated confidence and repeated voting missed correlated errors. Ask the compact contrastive prompt and short evidence-only blind1 reviewer. If both valid labels agree, keep the compact answer; otherwise use the rich base1 answer. Failure of the fallback remains an operational error. This does not make the two views independent. Compare with single, contrastive, and compact-first confidence routing at fixed 0.90. Target: at least 40% fewer recorded input tokens than single, at least 98% correct-edge retention, and no additional wrong edges. Any failed conjunct falsifies the operational target.

## H2: Dependency-safe fallback for invalid typed checks

Motivation: malformed check vectors made the original targeted pipeline fail even when a valid standalone judgment existed. Run base1; if invalid, use contrastive. Otherwise run checks; if invalid, keep the valid base1 and do not consume adjudication. With valid prerequisites, accept valid adjudication, or fall back to base1 if adjudication fails. Never renormalize malformed vectors, salvage a downstream decision from invalid prerequisites, or infer an unobserved new response. Compare with original targeted and single. Target: at least 75% fewer operational errors than targeted, at least 98% of single correct edges, and no more wrong edges than single. Mutation tests must show invalid prerequisites cannot authorize dependent answers.

## H3: Development-learned redundancy-capped voting

Motivation: identical wrong answers from repeat calls are not corroboration. Use base1/base2/base3/blind1/blind2/structured/contrastive. On development only, connect two views when their same-wrong-label intersection divided by the union of their error cases is at least 0.80, requiring a nonempty error union. Connected components are empirical redundancy clusters, not independent evidence sources. Each cluster casts at most one label vote from its mean valid distribution; require every component to have a valid view. A strict component majority and at least two supporting components are needed for a positive edge. Otherwise abstain, except a strict negative majority may return NOT_ENOUGH_INFO. Compare with flat seven-view majority and the single baseline at equal accepted volume. Target: at least 20% fewer wrong edges than flat voting at matched volume and at least 95% of single correct edges at natural volume. Include the one-cluster negative control. All seven calls are charged; no calibrated confidence or source independence claim.

## H4: Group-aware review allocation

Motivation: edge-average precision can hide contaminated source groups. Estimate edge error risk from development using (single label, single score >= 0.90, disagreement with contrastive), with two pseudo-observations shrunk to the development edge error rate (itself Laplace-smoothed). Compare descending individual risk with greedy maximum estimated reduction in source-group contamination, r_i times the product of (1-r_j) for other unreviewed edges in the group. This product is a heuristic, not a dependence guarantee. Primary budget is 20 accepted edges; also report budgets 10, 30 and 40. The primary simulated reviewer removes wrong edges and retains correct ones; no missing edge is added. Target: strictly fewer contaminated source groups than individual-risk review at budget 20, with no lower correct-edge retention. Also report analytic expected outcomes for reviewer sensitivity 0.50/0.75/1.00 and false-removal rates 0/0.01/0.05. These are reviewer-model sensitivity analyses, not observed human-review results or a database topology benchmark. Fixed-budget selection uses no test labels.

## H5: Joint conflict-component optimization

Motivation: the previous scope/interval guard detects conflicts but does not choose a mutually consistent batch; greedy insertion is order-sensitive. Build the declared conflict graph, split it into connected components, and choose a maximum-weight independent set by exhaustive search for components of at most 16 assertions. Stage larger components and malformed assertions rather than silently using an unsafe approximation. Weights are nonnegative supplied priorities, not certified truth probabilities. Compare with priority-first and input-order greedy on 128 seeded eight-assertion fixtures plus an explicit greedy counterexample. Use an independent oracle that enumerates active facts at integer instants, not the production pairwise conflict function. Target: zero conflicts or oracle-optimality failures, no utility below priority-greedy, and strict utility improvement on at least 10% of the 128 random fixtures. Check input-order invariance and fail-closed bounds. Include a misleading-weight negative control showing constraint consistency does not imply semantic truth. Controlled fixture counts are not independent semantic observations.

## Reporting and limits

Produce machine-readable results, per-candidate policy outputs, review selections, controlled fixture outcomes, CSV summaries, five SVG/PNG figures, a paper section and updated full Markdown/HTML/PDF. Record source and output SHA-256 hashes and runtime versions. Reproduce the original frozen study and existing extension tests. Do not change the default graph compiler, promote a policy, claim superiority to KARMA, equate Jev confidence with calibrated risk, or claim open-ended graph-generation accuracy. Primary sources motivating the scope: TypeSafe AI documentation (https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction) and KARMA (https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06472); neither is a rerun baseline here.
