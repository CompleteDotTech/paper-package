## H1: Source-balanced empirical-reliability ranking

Hypothesis: label-conditional empirical reliability can rank accepted edges better than raw confidence without another model call. For single-rich positive predictions, bin by predicted polarity and score in [0,.90), [.90,.99), [.99,1]. Each development source group has total edge weight one. Estimate each bin's correctness with four global-mean pseudo-observations; global mean has Beta(1,1) smoothing. Unseen bins use that global mean. Rank by estimated reliability, raw confidence, then ID. This is ranking, not a calibrated-probability claim. At K=145 test edges, require at least 20% fewer wrong edges than raw-confidence top-K, at least 95% retention of natural single-rich correct edges, and no fewer represented source groups. K=100 and 125 are diagnostics. Invalid/nonpositive predictions cannot enter either ranking. Include unweighted-fit ablation. No parameter selection on test outcomes.

## H2: Source-diverse equal-budget acceptance

Hypothesis: diminishing returns within a source improve source coverage without worsening edge quality. Greedily select the positive edge maximizing raw score divided by (1 + already selected count in its source group), then raw score and ID. At fixed K=100 require at least 10% more represented source groups, at least 98% of confidence-top-K correct edges, and no extra contaminated groups. K=125 and 145 are diagnostics. Compare natural and matched-budget results. Source coverage is NOT graph-node recall, connected-component recovery or candidate-generation recall.

## H3: Dependence-robust review allocation

Hypothesis: optimizing the union-bound upper envelope is more useful than the previous independent-risk objective. Reuse development-only fitted edge risks, then minimize sum over groups min(1, sum of residual edge risks). Reviewed risks are multiplied by (1-sensitivity), with sensitivity .75. For each group and allocated count, choose highest risks; exact group-budget dynamic programming allocates the budget. Compare the prior independent-risk optimal allocator at budgets 10,20,30,40. Primary at budget 20: strictly fewer expected contaminated observed-label groups, no lower expected correct edges (false removal .05), and no worse modeled union upper bound. Separately require zero optimality failures versus subset enumeration on 64 seeded eight-edge fixtures. Report lower envelope max residual marginal, independent estimate and upper envelope; enumerate 64 four-edge joint distributions to verify arbitrary-dependence containment. A deliberately miscalibrated marginal control must demonstrate that dependence robustness does not repair wrong marginals. Simulated reviewer sensitivities {.5,.75,1} and false-removal rates {0,.01,.05} are diagnostics, not trials.

## H4: Bounded exact shared-lineage probabilities

Hypothesis: memoized Shannon decomposition of OR-of-AND lineage can recover valid high-probability admissions lost by the earlier loose component bounds. Canonicalize/deduplicate proofs, remove absorbed supersets, condition on the most frequent atom (lexical tie), memoize residual formulas. Primitive Bernoulli events are supplied independent; proofs sharing atoms are NOT independent. Limit to 256 atoms, 512 input proofs and 4096 unique residual states; on exhaustion return prior valid bounds with explicit nonexact status, never a guessed probability. Primary: zero deviations over 128 seeded cases with eight atoms versus full possible-world enumeration (tolerance 1e-10), duplicate/permutation invariance, all eight shared-hub fixtures (8,16,32,64,96,128,192,256 atoms) exact against an analytic oracle, and at least one additional correct admission at threshold .95 with no false admissions. Include state-budget exhaustion and corrupted-lineage negative controls. This is exact probability conditional on supplied lineage/reliabilities, not factual truth or calibrated Jev confidence.

## H5: Bounded feedback-cutset conflict optimization

Hypothesis: conditioning on a small feedback vertex set extends exact weighted independent-set processing beyond forests. For each component at most 256 vertices, repeatedly remove a deterministic maximum-degree vertex from the current cycle-containing 2-core until a forest remains, with cutset cap four. Enumerate all independent cutset assignments, remove neighbors of selected cutset vertices, and invoke existing exact forest DP on the remainder. If cutset discovery exceeds four, retain prior solver fallback (small-component exact enumeration, otherwise explicit staging). Primary: zero oracle/consistency failures on 128 seeded weighted ten-vertex graphs, no utility regression versus the prior solver, deterministic order invariance, and exact unstaged solutions on sixteen large uniform/weighted cycles of sizes 17,24,32,48,64,96,128,256 against an independent path-recurrence cycle oracle. Include 17-clique and 257-cycle staging controls, and a high-priority false assertion control. Generic graph fixtures do not establish that document extraction produces those topologies.
