## Evidence and novelty boundary

The latest reliability study found that exact independent-primitive lineage still overstates confidence when independence is wrong, a maximum-priority conflict solver can choose a false assertion, review priorities optimize source contamination rather than downstream answers, and graph maintenance correctness depends on explicit dependency semantics. This extension tests five different responses to those limitations. Every new benchmark is a controlled algorithmic or decision-theoretic fixture. **Fresh Jev calls: zero. New independently adjudicated scientific documents: zero. Production-policy changes: zero.** Do not pool fixture counts with model observations, claim measured human-review benefit, or claim improved extraction accuracy.

The reviewed package protocols cover calibration, routing, agreement, graph-risk budgets, identity, provenance, interval conflicts, review allocation, independent-event lineage, one-best conflict solving and incremental component maintenance. The specific five mechanisms below were not found in that review. This is bounded package-level novelty, not evidence that nobody has attempted the ideas. Consistent query answering, probabilistic logic, value of information, deletion propagation and least-fixed-point inference are established prior art. New contributions are the implementations, assumption controls and linked experiments for this package; no globally unprecedented algorithm claim is authorized.

## H1: Dependence-agnostic lineage admission

Hypothesis: replacing an unjustified product-world assumption with bounds over all joint distributions compatible with supplied primitive marginals prevents correlation-induced false admissions. For a monotone DNF of sufficient proofs, enumerate worlds for at most eight distinct atoms and solve two linear programs over nonnegative world masses: normalization and supplied one-atom marginals are equality constraints; minimize/maximize the probability of the DNF. Canonicalize duplicate and subsumed proofs. Validate finite inputs, numerical feasibility and solver success. Numerical bounds carry a 1e-8 outward allowance; they are not formal real-arithmetic certificates. Above the atom cap or on solver failure, use dependency-free Frechet/union bounds on conjunctions and their disjunction, never an independence estimate.

Compare to exact independent-world evaluation and the dependency-free analytic fallback. Execute 128 seeded joint-distribution fixtures with two to six atoms, including sparse correlated worlds; report actual joint probability, independent estimate, both bounds and interval width. Check 16 two/three-atom cases against a separate rational vertex-enumeration oracle. Check duplicate/order invariance and threshold 0.95. Primary: zero containment/oracle violations beyond 1e-8; zero lower-bound false admissions on these fixtures; prevent the analytic shared-source false admission (two 0.8 marginal events, identical in reality: independent OR=0.96, actual=0.8); retain a valid singleton admission at 0.99. Include bad-marginal, malformed-input and over-cap controls. Correctness depends on accurate marginals and complete supplied proofs; no raw Jev score is treated as a calibrated source probability.

## H2: Skeptical optimal-repair backbone

Hypothesis: admitting only assertions present in every maximum-priority consistent repair avoids treating an arbitrary optimum tie-break as evidence. Input is a supplied undirected conflict graph and nonnegative integer priorities. For components of at most 16 vertices, use memoized maximum-weight independent-set recurrence and constrained re-optimization to compute necessary, possible and excluded assertions, with a witness optimum including/excluding every ambiguous assertion. Larger components stage; this is a new ambiguity capability, not a scalability improvement over the preceding cutset solver.

Compare the backbone with one deterministic optimum and independent exhaustive enumeration on 128 seeded graphs with two to twelve vertices. Execute four input-order permutations per case. Primary: zero necessary/possible/utility or witness mismatches, no loss relative to the unique optimum when unique, no arbitrary member admitted from an equal-priority conflicting pair, and preservation of an isolated required assertion. Report admitted-priority/coverage loss, ambiguity frequency and the 32-cycle unsupported control. The deliberately false assertion of priority 9 against true priority 8 remains necessary in the unique optimal repair: skepticism about ties is not semantic verification.

## H3: Query-loss-directed evidence review

Hypothesis: review selection aimed at downstream Boolean-query loss is more useful than ranking primitive error risk. Use three monotone DNF queries over six to eight independent primitive events with supplied probabilities. Review reveals a primitive perfectly at equal unit cost. Choose a nonadaptive subset of budget one or two minimizing expected residual sum of query Brier risks (conditional Bernoulli variance). Enumerate all subsets under a fixed eight-atom cap, no hidden truth labels. Compare with highest primitive-error probability and highest primitive entropy; also report no review. Evaluate 64 seeded fixtures. A separate direct world/observation Brier calculation verifies the selected loss and optimum.

Primary: zero oracle/regret failures within 1e-10 AND at least 20% lower aggregate residual query Brier loss than primitive-risk ranking at budget two. Keep the outcome even if the efficiency target fails. Report selected IDs, all subset scores, both budgets and paired fixture differences; any bootstrap interval is descriptive of this artificial generator, not a deployment guarantee. Include a misspecified-prior control and noisy-review sensitivity. Perfect review, known query lineage and correct probabilistic inputs are explicit assumptions; costs are review counts, not invented time or money.

## H4: Minimum-collateral assertion retraction plans

Hypothesis: provenance-guided hitting-set search can invalidate a designated erroneous conclusion while preserving protected conclusions and avoiding the collateral damage of withdrawing every supporting candidate assertion. Inputs are complete monotone proof DNFs over at most 14 candidate-commitment atoms, positive integer withdrawal costs, an externally designated target and protected conclusions. The output is a proposed staging/retraction plan, never deletion of source documents. Search hits every target proof, preserves at least one proof for each protected conclusion, and lexicographically minimizes (number of other lost conclusions, withdrawal cost, number of withdrawals, sorted IDs). Bounded unsupported or infeasible inputs stage without mutation.

Execute 128 seeded fixtures over four to ten atoms and six conclusions. Compare with withdraw-the-union, a cost-first greedy hitting set and independent enumeration of every withdrawal subset. Primary: zero feasibility/objective mismatches; no protected loss; at least 25% lower mean collateral than union withdrawal on cases where both return a feasible plan (if its aggregate collateral is zero, the improvement target fails). Report feasibility/coverage on all cases separately so staging cannot masquerade as quality. Include impossible protection, an omitted alternative proof, incorrect target adjudication, an over-cap input and permutation invariance. Minimal intervention is not proof that the chosen candidate assertions are factually false.

## H5: Grounded recursive evidence materialization

Hypothesis: least-fixed-point grounding removes self-supporting inference cycles after external support is withdrawn while retaining conclusions with an alternative grounded proof. Inputs are finite ground positive Horn rules and explicit base assertions; no negation, function symbols or ontology induction. A worklist with body counters starts only from current base assertions and fires grounded rules. Compare with naive support-count deletion from the previous materialization, which can retain cycles, and an independent finite-model oracle: enumerate every interpretation satisfying all rules and bases and intersect them.

Execute 96 seeded four-to-eight-atom rule programs, each with 12 base snapshots (1,152 snapshot evaluations), including additions and withdrawals. Primary: zero finite-model-oracle mismatches; remove every unsupported assertion in externally seeded cycles of sizes 2,4,8,16,32,64 after the only seed is removed; retain the independently anchored alternative; eliminate at least one phantom assertion left by naive support counts. Report rule/body processing work without equating it to service latency. Include unsupported cyclic proofs with no seed, duplicate/order invariance and a false-base semantic control. Grounded entailment is truth only relative to the supplied bases and rules; this prototype does not verify their real-world correctness.
